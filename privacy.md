# Privacy Policy — Channel Upload Tool

Last updated: 2026-11-07

This is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels **Ollie Octopus World**, **Fragile Giants** and **컷푸리 (@cutpuri)** to manage those channels. It is not offered to the public.

## What the tool accesses
When the owner signs in with Google, the tool requests:
- `youtube.upload` and `youtube` — to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channels and update their settings.
- `yt-analytics.readonly` — to read view and audience statistics for the owner's own channels.

## How the data is used
- Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to produce private performance reports for the owner.
- Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- No data is collected from any other person.

## Storage
OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer and server and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Contact
Questions can be sent through the contact options on the channels listed above.
